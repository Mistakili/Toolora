### Can You Replicate Toolora on Solana?
**Yes, with minimal changes.**

Your current setup (Express backend + x402 middleware + Stellar mainnet payments) is **chain-agnostic** at its core. The x402 protocol itself is designed to work across multiple chains.

**How to port it**:
- Use the official/community **Solana x402 packages**:
  - `@x402/express` (same middleware you already use)
  - `@x402/svm` or `@x402/core` + Solana-specific verifier (for SPL tokens like USDC)
  - There are also dedicated ones like `solana-pay-x402` middleware that support both programmatic x402 flows and Solana Pay QR.
- Change the payment config:
  - Instead of XLM on Stellar, use **USDC** (most popular) or any SPL/Token-2022 token.
  - Update your wallet address to a Solana one.
  - Verification switches to Solana RPC (e.g., Helius, QuickNode, or public RPC) instead of Stellar Horizon.
- Pricing: You can keep similar micro amounts (e.g., 0.0001–0.01 USDC per call) because Solana fees are extremely low (~$0.00025 per tx).
- Discovery endpoints (`/api/agent` and `/.well-known/agent.json`) stay almost identical — just update the network and token details.

Many devs already do exactly this: one codebase with config for Stellar **or** Solana (or even multi-chain). Your PDF/text and URL/read endpoints would work the same way — just swap the payment verifier.

**Advantages on Solana**:
- **Much higher agent activity**: Solana currently handles **50–65%+** of all x402 transactions and a huge share of volume (tens of millions of txs, $10M+ processed on Solana alone since launch).
- Faster and cheaper for high-frequency calls (sub-second finality).
- Better liquidity in **USDC** — agents prefer stablecoins over volatile tokens like SOL or XLM for payments.
- Strong ecosystem support (Solana docs have x402 guides, Coinbase pushed it hard on Solana).

**Disadvantages**:
- Slightly more competition (more agents and services already on Solana).
- You’d need a Solana wallet and RPC access (easy and cheap).

Overall, replicating is straightforward and low-risk — you could even run **both Stellar and Solana versions** in parallel from the same backend with route-based config.
